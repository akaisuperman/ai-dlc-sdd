# Brown-field Analysis

Phan tich codebase hien co de tao context cho aiteam workflow. Ap dung khi du an da co code va can them feature hoac refactor.

## Khi nao dung

- Du an da co codebase, can them feature moi
- Can refactor module cu
- Tiep nhan du an tu team khac
- Can hieu he thong truoc khi viet spec

## Quy trinh

Xem chi tiet: [references/analysis-guide.md](references/analysis-guide.md)

## Output

Ket qua brown-field analysis duoc luu vao `docs/ai/specs/overview.md` (phan kien truc hien tai) va cac module specs tuong ung.

## Nguyen tac

- **Doc code truoc, hoi sau** — hieu cau truc truoc khi hoi ve business logic
- **Map truoc, chi tiet sau** — bat dau tu high-level (folders, entry points), di sau dan
- **Khong refactor khi phan tich** — muc dich la hieu, khong phai sua
- **Ghi lai tat ca assumptions** — nhung dieu gia dinh can xac nhan voi team/owner cu
